| BUG-002 | M06 coal winch — cannot reverse from near track endpoint | **Owner playtest (2026-10-13):** with a gripped freight cart at the *near/winch end* of the track, attempts to select FEED instead repeatedly select PULL; the lever immediately returns to NEUTRAL and the cart is stuck until broken/reset. Normal cycling works away from the endpoint. | **Likely cause, source-inspected (not yet independently reproduced):** `CoalWinch.cycleLever` always advances `NEUTRAL → PULL → FEED`, while `CableDrive.stop` reports `track_end`/`end` for impossible PULL motion and `tickWinch` immediately forces NEUTRAL. Claude to choose a simple, legible control fix; test both endpoints, repeated interactions, reversals, unloading and dock behavior in real client plus regression tests. Do not solve by allowing movement off the track or by deleting the safety stop. | **FIX IMPLEMENTED AND TESTED** in M06 `fc8f99a`, Build #20 plus Build #23 regression. **M06 OWNER-PASSED 2026-10-13**; no separate human re-reproduction asserted.
