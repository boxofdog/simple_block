| BUG-002 | M06 coal winch — cannot reverse from near track endpoint | **Owner playtest (2026-10-13):** with a gripped freight cart at the *near/winch end* of the track, attempts to select FEED instead repeatedly select PULL; the lever immediately returns to NEUTRAL and the cart is stuck until broken/reset. Normal cycling works away from the endpoint. | **Likely cause, source-inspected (not yet independently reproduced):** `CoalWinch.cycleLever` always advances `NEUTRAL → PULL → FEED`, while `CableDrive.stop` reports `track_end`/`end` for impossible PULL motion and `tickWinch` immediately forces NEUTRAL. Do not solve by allowing movement off the track or by deleting the safety stop. | **OPEN — M06 owner playtest regression; resolve before M06 owner acceptance and M07 integration.** *Claude, 2026-10-13: fix in [`fc8f99a`](https://github.com/boxofdog/simple_block/commit/fc8f99a529225230f62742837fae505212c6468e). After a directional stop, the lever's next click skips the blocked way, so at the near end NEUTRAL goes to FEED. Reproduced by WIN-06 (fails without the fix), checked at both ends and in the real client, CI Build #20 green. Left open for the owner to confirm in game.* |
