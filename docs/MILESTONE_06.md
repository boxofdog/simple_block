# Simple Block — Milestone 06: Freight Handling, Coal-Powered Winch, Recipe Simplification and Creative Cleanup (DRAFT)

**Status: Design scaffold — NOT authorized for implementation.** Owner direction remains incomplete. **M05 is committed and independently verified on GitHub/CI, but owner acceptance is still pending.** Refine this document together; do not ask Claude to execute it as-is or infer that M06 is authorized.

## Purpose

Build the first physical **freight-transfer railway interface**, add a **coal-powered upgrade to the existing manual winch**, **simplify existing cable and cable-coil recipes**, and **clean retired items out of normal Creative inventory/search**, preparing the mine → skip → railway → smelter loop. Preserve the ordinary vanilla minecart as Simple Block's freight cart. The special loading track *is* the future transfer station: no compulsory secondary loading/unloading station block or item-conveyor system. The powered winch must improve the SAME M05 cable system, **not introduce a parallel machine network**. Actual upright mining skips and their transfer/hoisting gameplay are planned for **M07**; the large smelter is **M08** and the full armor overhaul **M09**, all requiring later authorization.

## 1. Loading track (owner-specified direction)

- Introduce a **loading track**: a rail section that **locks a freight minecart in position** for cargo handling, rather than letting it drift or be shoved away while a transfer is attempted.
- The **loading track itself determines the orientation of the freight cart's front**. The cart's approach direction does **not** choose which end opens. It locks in place and selects a cargo-transfer state from its **relative position to the skip**:
  - **Skip below the track (cart → skip):** when the space in front of the oriented cart/track is clear for deployment, the cart's **front panel unfolds into a downward ramp** that directs bulk ore into the skip's open upper compartment. This is the **loading-the-skip** state. Retract the ramp when this mode ends or the cart is released.
  - **Skip beside the track (skip → cart):** the cart's front **stays closed**. The aligned skip's built-in lower chute and automatic discharge gate feed ore into the stationary cart. The track/cart **accepts freight without opening its panel**.
  - Do not open the cart or discharge items merely because an empty space exists in front of the rail. The mode requires a compatible positional relationship with the skip. A blocked ramp must not clip through other blocks; do not lose or duplicate cargo.
- Keep the **same vanilla minecart and its 256-item, single-type inventory**; these are state-dependent animations/handling rules, not a new freight-cart item/entity.
- **Shapeless crafting recipe:** **one vanilla rail + one existing copper cable anchor → Loading Track**. They can occupy **any two crafting-grid positions**; the number of loading-track items produced per craft is **not yet specified**. Reuse the anchor item as an ingredient; do not create a new anchor variant.
- This rail is the intended physical interface for **cart ↔ upright skip** transfers and, later, mine-to-smelter logistics. The cart's ramp into a skip **below** and the skip's built-in chute into a cart **beside** are distinct modes of the *same rail*, not distinct station machines. The full skip and bulk-smelter implementations remain outside initial M06 scope unless subsequently authorized. **No separate compulsory unloading station or conveyor belt.**
- **SETTLED:** one rail and one copper anchor in a shapeless recipe; the rail orients the cart front; cart-to-skip-below deploys a downward ramp if unobstructed; skip-beside-to-cart keeps the cart closed; the rail itself defines the transfer station.
- **NOT YET SETTLED:** recipe output count; exact rail facing/configuration gesture and in-world model; lock/release controls; connection to an active train; transfer triggers/timing and validation; compatibility with future visible cargo fill, rod and cushioning; exact world clearance and relative-position tolerances. Keep the ramp/cargo transfer safe and explicit rather than assuming opening means instant lossless unloading.

## 2. Retire redstone rails (owner-specified direction)

- Remove **redstone-dependent rail variants specifically**, provisionally meaning **powered rails, detector rails and activator rails**. **Ordinary vanilla rails remain**; do not remove useful non-rail redstone-adjacent blocks as collateral.
- No redstone-based rail propulsion/control should be necessary: Cobblestone Engines and cable winches provide motive power, and the loading track provides physical stopping/loading infrastructure.
- Before implementation, audit recipes, loot/structure generation, item acquisition, placed-block behavior, existing M05 train tests, rail switches and cart variants to decide precisely how each redstone rail is retired (availability, behavior and compatibility). **The three-variant interpretation and removal mechanics should be confirmed by the owner before code work.**
- Do not silently replace redstone rail signals with an elaborate mechanical/electrical automation network.

## 3. Hand-pushing freight carts (owner-specified direction)

- **A minecart that cannot carry a passenger is not manually pushable by a player.** This includes the ordinary uncushioned freight cart: player-body contact/shoving should not impart cart motion. Do not substitute another hidden hand-push gesture.
- **Cushioned passenger carts remain manually movable** (retain practical player interaction). Freight carts must still move under **Cobblestone Engine traction, coupling, cable haulage and gravity** as designed; this is not an instruction to immobilize freight or block ordinary world physics.
- Clarify whether the powered Cobblestone Engine is subject to exactly the same no-player-push rule, and ensure protections do not break M05 movement, loading into tracks, or train physics.

## 4. Coal-powered winch upgrade (M06 design requirement; mechanical details OPEN)

- **Owner has added the coal-powered winch to M06's intended scope.** The existing hand-operated M05 winch/drum gains a **localized coal-fueled drive**, usable for the same cable-hauling system and later M07 upright skip hoisting. This is **not** a request for a new power-grid, generic rotary-shaft network or unrelated factory tech tree.
- **Owner-settled modular construction:** apply a **vanilla furnace directly to an EXISTING M05 winch drum** to upgrade it to a coal-powered winch. This parallels furnace-on-minecart and cushions-on-minecart: improve established machinery rather than create a new item/system. **The furnace visibly mounts on the free side of the drum AND the old manual crank is replaced by a directional LEVER.** Preserve all installed cable, anchors and connections; the unupgraded manual winch retains its crank and M05 controls. Exact hit zones, furnace item consumption, appearance and upgrade/save-load safety remain implementation details. No separate generator.
- **Three-position latching lever (owner-settled):** upgrading the existing drum replaces the manual crank with one **PULL / NEUTRAL / FEED lever**. **PULL points BACKWARD** and hauls toward the drum, **FEED points FORWARD** and moves cable away, and **NEUTRAL (center) turns the drive OFF and holds the load**. **A single right-click on the LEVER advances exactly one setting in a repeating three-state cycle**; the lever **latches at that position**, rather than springing to neutral when the mouse is released. **A selected PULL or FEED setting runs the motor on its own while the physical haul can continue**; Steve may leave after setting it. This supersedes the earlier continuous-player-hold/proximity assumption. **Automatic physical stops (cart locking at loading track, derailment/path ending, M07 skip reaching top/bottom or pausing at a compatible station) immediately flip the visible lever back to NEUTRAL**, turn off motion and pause fuel consumption. The winch **never self-restarts** or automatically repeats a trip. **When currently NEUTRAL, the first right-click selects PULL (backward) by default.** The subsequent click-cycle transition order remains to be specified; no fourth position, spring return, separate run button, global automation or power network. The unupgraded manual winch retains its M05 hold-left/feed and hold-right/pull crank controls. Closed cable loops retain constant installed length.
- **Simple coal interactions (owner-settled):** **right-click the upgraded winch with coal held** to add coal to its internal fuel supply; **right-click it with an empty hand** to inspect **how much coal remains inside**. No extra fuel hopper, furnace inventory GUI or dedicated fueling machine is required by this design. Exact fuel-storage capacity, insertion stack count, and player-facing count presentation (text vs inventory display) remain implementation details. **Distinguish interaction with the drum/furnace body from operating the directional lever** so empty-hand inspection does not accidentally set the winch moving. Regular coal is explicitly supported; other furnace fuels are not implicitly approved.
- **Powered operation and burn stopping (owner-settled):** coal-powered hauling is **faster and more fuel-efficient** than manual winching. While lever is latched PULL or FEED **and the load is physically moving**, consume fuel. **NEUTRAL or any real halt immediately stops motion/combustion**: right-clicking to neutral, derailment or rail-path end, a minecart stopped/locked at loading track, or (M07) a skip at shaft top/bottom or stopping at a compatible intermediate transfer track. **At a physical end-of-path/transfer stop, automatically flip the lever back to NEUTRAL; it must not remain latched and unexpectedly resume.** Keep load, installed cable and queued unspent coal. An active partly spent fuel unit retains its **exact remaining burn time** through any stop, idle time or save/load and resumes only after the operator explicitly sets a running lever position again and load actually moves; never grant a fresh fuel unit or consume it twice. A blocked route/overload should fail safe without burning coal or silently bypassing the stop.
- **M07 docking interface that M06 must support (NOT M06 skip implementation):** a **loaded** skip can stop mid-shaft when a compatible **not-full freight cart is waiting at a loading track** to receive ore via the skip's lower chute. An **empty** skip meeting a suitable freight-cart/loading-track arrangement treats the cart as **a source feeding the skip from above**. Station direction follows cargo state and physical geometry; no wall-crossing transfers. **Any such stop flips the powered winch lever to NEUTRAL and pauses the coal burn**, and the operator must right-click the lever again to select a drive direction for further movement. No autonomous return trips or automated restart.
- **Still open for an M06 executable brief:** remaining three-position right-click transitions **after the settled NEUTRAL → PULL default** (while respecting one-position-per-click), coal capacity and insertion quantity (coal established; other fuels not assumed), inspection count presentation, quantitative speed/haulage and coal efficiency, model/texture, lever versus winch-body hit zones, fuel-empty behavior, safe collision/derailment/overload behavior, break/retrieval, multiplayer and save/load. Persist lever state and remaining active coal burn with no motion or burn on loading an idling/neutral winch. No power network, remote operation commands or recurrent logistics automation. Exact remaining burn-time preservation is settled.
- **M06 acceptance when authorized:** test furnace-on-existing-drum preserving cable and changing crank to the **three-position lever**; each **right-click on lever changes exactly one position**; PULL/backward and FEED/forward move opposite ways and **remain running without holding the mouse**; NEUTRAL stops/holds; fuel right-click on winch body inserts coal and empty-hand right-click body inspects amount without touching lever. Test that **cart docking, derailment/path end and blocked motion automatically set lever to NEUTRAL and stop coal burn**, with no unintended restart; ensure the correct pause/resume of partially consumed coal across save/load without cargo, cable or fuel duplication. Verify dedicated server, multiplayer and real client. The M07 skip entity and transfer code stay outside M06; test their future auto-neutral contract as far as possible without implementing skips.

## 6. Cable and cable-coil crafting simplification (M06 owner-specified)

**Replace the existing M04 crafting ingredient layouts** for the already-registered cable and cable coil items. Both are **shaped recipes**, so ingredient alignment matters. The **center crafting column** must contain exactly the three listed items, with all six other cells empty. This is a recipe revision, not a new kind of cable or coil.

**Cable** — three ordinary **iron nuggets** vertically in the center column; **remove string** as an ingredient:

```text
[empty] [iron nugget] [empty]
[empty] [iron nugget] [empty]
[empty] [iron nugget] [empty]
```

**Cable Coil** — three existing **cable items** vertically in the center column; replace the current eight-cable ring:

```text
[empty] [cable] [empty]
[empty] [cable] [empty]
[empty] [cable] [empty]
```

**Known existing implementation (M04–M05, unchanged until M06 authorization):** three iron nuggets **plus string** shapelessly craft **two cable items**; each cable item represents **four blocks** of cable; eight cable items in a ring produce one **32-block coil**, and unpacking a coil gives back eight cable items.

**Yield and length conservation require resolution before coding:** the owner has specified the **ingredients and their positions**, NOT any new output counts or cable-length conversion. In particular, replacing the eight-cable coil craft with a three-cable coil craft while keeping the current 8-cable uncoiling recipe would **duplicate cable**, which is unacceptable. Finalize cable output count; coil output count; whether one coil's physical length becomes **12 blocks** (three existing 4-block cable items) or another explicitly chosen length; the reverse coil→cable recipe and its output; and any related drum capacity, copper/iron heavy weights, UI text or existing M04/M05 conservation tests. Do not silently change these values or change the physical cable item identity.

**M06 acceptance when authorized:** positive and negative shaped-grid craft tests; confirm no string is required; iron nuggets/cables **only in the stated center-column slots**; remove old ring/shapeless crafting paths; test crafting/unpacking round trips for no loss/duplication; confirm winch load, route/disconnect/dismantle, drum removal and save/load conserve installed cable length with the finalized values.
