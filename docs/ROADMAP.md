## M09 — Threats to defended settlements (planned; not authorized)

Build on existing light entrancement and physical defenses without raids, invasion waves, or new mob species. Monsters should opportunistically exploit newly reachable illuminated areas when a wall, gate, or door opens or is breached: use their existing memory of lit destinations, reevaluate paths after access changes, and temporarily favor reachable interior light before resuming normal loitering or player pursuit. Do not require monsters to identify abstract 'bases' or give them omniscient knowledge of entrances.   ```

- **Physical freight transfer direction (owner-settled M07):** the existing M06 loading track itself remains the station. **A freight cart adjacent to the collidable FOURTH-block BRIDLE is the SOURCE, dumping through its opened front ramp into the skip's top opening**. **A freight cart adjacent to the FIRST/BOTTOM block of the three-block skip (the lower chute section) is the RECIPIENT**, taking cargo from the skip's own lower chute; the cart front remains closed. **Determine this mode from skip section + valid docking geometry**, not from the presence of an empty/blocked space in front of the cart, and **not merely from whether the skip is currently empty or loaded**. Clearance/collision still veto unsafe motion and door deployment but do not choose direction. Commodity/type/capacity decide whether transfer is possible, not which way it goes. No separate external chute or station, wall-crossing transfer, or dropped/duplicated goods. **Safe transfer timing and exact rate are Claude's discretion.**
- **Cable attachment and collidable bridle:** using the existing loose cable hook on the skip connects a triangular two-leg suspension bridle, attached to either side of the top ore intake and meeting at one apex. The **bridle is physically collidable in the FOURTH block above the skip base**, moving with the skip rather than ghosting through terrain or the loading track. **ONE main hoisting cable**, using M06's unlimited-length reusable installation, runs from the apex over the existing guide wheel to the winch. Do not create two independent main hoisting lines or invent an extra material-length charge. Claude determines safe precise bridle hitbox/interaction and keeps owner-painted assets editable.
- **Vertical motion (owner decision):** the skip moves **straight up or down directly beneath its hoist wheel**, using the **normal power/speed of its connected winch** through the single hoisting cable and bridle. It remains upright, with **no swinging, pendulum physics, or mandatory guide rails/timber-lined shaft system**. Ordinary clearance/collision with the surrounding world still matters; do not add a separate skip-only drive or new guidance machinery.
- **M05 hand crank versus M06 latching powered lever (owner decision):** on the **unupgraded M05 drum**, empty-handed **hold left-click = FEED/away** and **hold right-click = PULL/toward**; release stops. Adding a furnace to the installed drum in M06 **replaces the crank with a three-position lever**: **PULL points BACKWARD, NEUTRAL (center) is OFF, FEED points FORWARD**. **Each right-click on the lever cycles it exactly one position**; the lever **stays at that position**, and PULL/FEED keep driving until the operator chooses NEUTRAL or a physical stop occurs. The player need not hold the click or remain next to the drum. **At a loading track, cart derailment/route end, or future M07 skip station/shaft end, the lever visibly flips to NEUTRAL, stopping/holding the drive and fuel burn; do not automatically restart it.** Right-click with coal on winch body adds fuel, bare-hand right-click body displays remaining coal, not lever control. Paused active coal retains its **exact remaining burn time** across stops and save/load, continuing only when motion resumes under a newly selected running state. Closed circulating loops keep total cable length fixed. **Complete right-click sequence settled: NEUTRAL → PULL → FEED → NEUTRAL; direct reverse must be safe.** M05 implements the manual crank; M06 the powered lever; M07 the skip.
- **Automatic skip stops and doors (owner-settled M07):** when an appropriately docked freight cart is adjacent to the skip's **FOURTH-block BRIDLE**, it is **feeding the skip** through the top opening; when adjacent to the **FIRST/BOTTOM skip block**, it is **receiving from the skip** through its lower chute. This section-based rule **supersedes the former provisional empty-skip/loaded-skip inference**. An empty source, full or wrong-commodity recipient, misalignment or obstruction means **no transfer**, not a direction reversal. A compatible intermediate dock or shaft endpoint stops/holds the skip and automatically flips any powered lever to **NEUTRAL**, pausing remaining fuel without self-restarting. A manually stopped skip likewise holds safely; doors should only deploy for genuinely compatible aligned transfer geometry. Claude chooses safe transfer rate, staging and exact docking tolerances without losing/duplicating cargo.
- **Alignment behavior and transfer timing delegated to Claude:** choose local loading-track search range, precise vertical tolerances, small safe snap/docking adjustments, transfer rate and animation timing. The **height of the matching skip section** fixes transfer direction: **bridle = cart dumps; first/bottom skip block = cart receives**. Verify clearance for the collidable fourth-block bridle and **one free lateral block on each side** of the wider sideways-minecart visual. Never transfer because a random nearby block is empty, move cargo through walls, or clip the skip through world blocks.
- **Not M05 or M06 implementation work.** M07 has **settled physical geometry** (1×1×3 body collision, collidable bridle above, lateral visual clearance), **one-type/one-special-slot 512-unit capacity**, and **section-defined transfer direction**; Claude handles timing and numeric alignment tolerances when M07 is separately authorized. No extra mandatory shaft guide system, new loading station or duplicate winch is required.

### Future bulk ore smelting and freight transfer (planned M08 blast furnace; not authorized)

- **Batch-based raw-material handling, not Create-style ore conveyors.** The practical mine-to-blast furnace loop should move bulk ore in minecarts or upright mining skips, then discharge it **by gravity into a raised receiving hopper** on a substantial, constructed blast furnace. No requirement to transport every individual ore item through exposed processing belts.
- **Two processing scales:** retain the ordinary furnace for early/small-scale iron and other familiar uses, but **slow its raw-ore smelting** so it does not compete with bulk processing. **Remove/disable the vanilla blast furnace** in a future authorized metallurgy milestone to avoid a redundant intermediate metal-processing shortcut.
- **Large blast furnace:** a visually substantial piece of settlement infrastructure with a hopper/charging point, a fuel demand, and a deliberate output or tapping point. Its functional reward is handling large quantities of ore efficiently, making rail elevation, terminal placement, vertical skips and a permanent mine-to-settlement supply line valuable.
- **Physical delivery:** design unloading from elevated rails and skips alongside the receiving hopper, not as independent item-teleportation or general automatic-factory systems. Material should never disappear or duplicate when an unloading attempt fails or the recipient is full.
- **M08 pre-industrial blast furnace recipe (OWNER-SETTLED, not yet implemented):** craft the **new standalone pre-industrial blast furnace** with the following exact **shaped 3×3 layout**, also accepting the **horizontal mirror** of the whole arrangement. All explicitly empty slots must remain empty; it is **not shapeless** and may **not rotate** by 90°/180°. `Iron block` means a normal vanilla **Block of Iron**; `iron ingot` means an ordinary vanilla **Iron Ingot**; `hopper`, `cauldron` and `furnace` are the existing vanilla items. No extra intermediates.

  ```text
  [empty]    [hopper]     [empty]
  [empty]    [iron block] [iron ingot]
  [cauldron] [furnace]    [iron ingot]
  ```

  **Accepted horizontal mirror:**

  ```text
  [empty]      [hopper]     [empty]
  [iron ingot] [iron block] [empty]
  [iron ingot] [furnace]    [cauldron]
  ```

  **Owner has settled ingredients/placement and mirroring, not the output count.** Use one blast furnace item per craft as a *proposed* conventional default when finalizing M08, unless owner specifies otherwise. Future M08 tests should accept either pictured arrangement and reject mirrored-by-rotation, misplaced ingredients and extraneous items. This is **M08 only**, not permission to implement it with M06.
- **Fuel and casting direction:** the pre-industrial blast furnace accepts **blocks of coal, charcoal, or dried kelp** as its only planned fuels. Coal blocks and dried kelp blocks are vanilla; a charcoal block would require a new item/recipe, to be scoped separately. Smelt raw iron, copper or gold into the corresponding **metal blocks**, preserving vanilla block-to-ingot crafting reversibility. No mandatory ore crushing or refinement stages.
- **Animated channel, practical mold inventory (owner decision):** show molten metal pouring down the front casting channel into a mold as an **animation only**, not a simulated fluid or a block the player must repeatedly break. **Interact with the mold to open/collect its output inventory; it stores at most one stack (64) of a single iron, gold or copper block type.** Finished blocks accumulate there automatically without requiring a click after every casting. No mixed block types in the same mold at once; when full or containing a different type, further output must wait safely rather than vanish. Preserve partial progress when raw-item quantities are not divisible by nine.
- **Open design questions before a milestone brief:** blast furnace structure/footprint, raw-input reservoir and hopper capacity, exact charcoal-block crafting rule, furnace slowdown, blast furnace fuel and processing rates, whether and how users switch between metal types, manual vs passive freight dumping, and later steelmaking integration. No conveyor networks or industrial process simulation by assumption.

## Working rules

- **Addition by subtraction:** prefer making existing Minecraft objects useful over adding redundant variants.
- **Useful infrastructure, not chores:** machinery should permanently improve what players can do.
- **One small milestone at a time:** focused scope, build/actual-client verification where appropriate, closeout report, then stop.
- **Editable assets:** preserve source Blockbench models and owner-authored textures.
- **Single source of truth:** milestone specs and reports hold implementation detail; this roadmap only tracks priorities and progress. Never silently promote backlog suggestions to requirements.
