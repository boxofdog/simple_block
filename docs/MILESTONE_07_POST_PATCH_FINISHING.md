## 2. Hoist cable must visibly MOVE when winching a SKIP

**Owner observation:** When the drum winches a skip, the skip changes height and the drum/spool may turn, but the visible cable appears **stationary along its length**. The owner wants the same obvious **moving-cable visual treatment as an M05/M06 winched minecart**, so one can see that the cable is being wound or paid out.

- Apply consistent, direction-correct **scrolling cable texture/strand motion** along the relevant overhead routed run **and the downward vertical segment** from ceiling pulley to skip bridle. PULL lifting the skip should read as cable traveling **toward the drum**, FEED lowering it as cable traveling **away**. The visual phase must advance from the **same actual cable-drive travel** as the animated drum (interpolated for partial ticks), not a time-only independent effect.
- The **real cable geometry and length still follow the moving skip and remain anchored through the actual supports/pulley**: as the skip rises or falls, its cable endpoint tracks the bridle, while texture/visible motion travels over the static world-space segments. Avoid the illusion of a fixed cable painted in the air, a cable sliding off the pulley, discontinuous UV motion at bends, inverted scrolling on the descending run, or jumping when reversal or partial ticks change.
- **When stopped, cable visibly stops**: NEUTRAL, station dock, blocked shaft, endpoints, no fuel, unloaded/non-ticking entity, save/reopen. Correct interpolation on start/reversal; no apparent travel when nothing is actually hauled. Reuse/adapt existing moving cable phase used on closed minecart cable loops in `CableDrumRenderer` / `CableGeometry`. `OreSkipRenderer` currently draws its cable using the unphased `CableGeometry.addRun` overload; maintain consistent motion across renderers without double-rendering the hanging section.
- The freshly fixed **winch-controlled free hook before skip attachment** should share the same consistent visual logic when lowering/raising if it follows naturally from the common cable path, without altering the prior patch's pulley conversion rule: **the top anchor stays an ordinary anchor until the free hook is successfully attached to a skip**. No new gameplay limits, inventories, systems, extra blocks, forced chunk loading, or unnecessary effects.

**Required proof:** Actual-client capture showing two or more **successive frames / a short recording while moving** (a static frame or assertion that the skip's Y changes does not prove the cable moves). Include upward and downward movement, reversal, stop/hold (no drifting), manual and powered drum, short and deep shafts, pulley bend and joined overhead line, coupled freight cart cable-loop regression and multiplayer state. The visual motion must match winch/spool direction and speed; preserve cargo, cable and coal conservation.
