# M16 — Village generation, size and settlement design overhaul

**Owner-directed early design, 2026-10-20. PLANNED - NOT READY; not implementation authorization.** First establish believable physical villages: larger buildings, reasonable terrain and connected space to support actual villager life. This milestone is the **physical foundation** for [M17](MILESTONE_17.md) jobs/commerce, [M18](MILESTONE_18.md) guardian patrols and [M19](MILESTONE_19.md) settlement wellbeing. It is not a fully specified world-generation handoff.

## Broad goals — physical villages

- **Sensible locations:** Avoid generating villages on extreme slopes, cliffs or jagged footprints that create floating houses, tall dirt pedestals, inaccessible doors and disconnected streets. Favor reasonably level continuous ground; workable gentle slopes and mountain plateaus may remain valid.
- **Larger, believable structures:** Make houses genuinely enterable, navigable and large enough for inhabitants. Build properly sized workshops, general stores and communal spaces instead of tiny playset structures.
- **Coherent layouts:** Connect homes, workshops, public spaces and the village center with accessible streets and paths; villagers and players should actually be able to move through buildings and between destinations.
- **Sensible population:** Match the number of residents and available home/workspace capacity to settlement size so villages feel inhabited rather than mostly empty.
- **Real community destinations:** Provide a physically usable **pub/tavern or other social meeting space**, large enough for a group to gather. The corresponding schedules and professional behaviors belong in M17.
- **Player-founded villages:** Favor designs and space conventions that can also inform the usable-housing/open-space eligibility checks in M19, without requiring player settlements to use a generated template.

## Boundaries

This milestone covers the **terrain footprint, architecture, street layout, usable interiors, population scale and physical communal buildings**. It does **not** introduce the shopkeeper/trade system, job services or detailed daily scheduling ([M17](MILESTONE_17.md)), improve Iron Golem AI ([M18](MILESTONE_18.md)), or impose happiness and resettlement requirements ([M19](MILESTONE_19.md)). Do not invent an autonomous village expansion simulator, mandatory building materials, new professions or progression mechanics.

## Open design decisions before READY

Village size ranges and biome distribution; terrain-suitability thresholds and failure behavior; building footprints/interiors, furniture and path geometry; population density; pub/community layout; compatibility with vanilla structures, fresh-world generation, performance and existing mods. Settle the recognizable architectural direction and bounded generation/testing plan with the owner before implementation.

**Lifecycle:** PLANNED - NOT READY. No M16 code, art or implementation authorization is claimed. M16 precedes M17, M18 and M19 in the planned village overhaul.
